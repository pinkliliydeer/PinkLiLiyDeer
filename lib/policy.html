Shipping Policy
Delivery Areas
Shipping is available to all governorates in Egypt.
Shipping Time
Orders are delivered within 3–7 business days depending on the governorate.
Shipping Fees
Shipping costs depend on your location and will be confirmed at the time of order.
Order Inspection Upon Delivery
Customers can inspect their order upon delivery for up to 10 minutes.
Important Notes
No changes or returns are accepted after the delivery person leaves.
Partial delivery is not available. The order must be received in full or returned in full, with shipping fees to be paid.
In case of any delays beyond Pink Liliy Deer's control, customers will be notified immediately.
